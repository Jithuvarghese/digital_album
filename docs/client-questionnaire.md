# Your Wedding Website: Questionnaire

Thank you for choosing us to create your wedding website. Your answers become the words and pictures your guests will see, so please write the way you speak: warm, personal, and in your own voice. Short answers are perfectly fine. Skip anything that doesn't apply.

You can answer in English, Malayalam, Tamil, or a mix. Let us know which languages you would like the website to show.

---

## 1. About the two of you

1. Your full names, and the names you'd like shown on the website (for example "Karthik & Anjali").
2. Nicknames family and friends use for each of you.
3. A short line about each of you (two or three sentences): what you do, where you're from, something that makes you *you*.
4. One fun fact about each of you that guests would enjoy.
5. Is there a line or phrase that sums up your wedding or your story? (For example: "Two families, one sadya, a lifetime of stories.")
6. A wedding hashtag, if you have one.

## 2. Your story

7. How did you first meet? Where, when, and what happened?
8. What were the first words you said to each other, or the first messages you exchanged? (We turn these into a "How we met" chat that guests reveal one message at a time, so the more real it feels, the better.)
9. List 4 to 6 milestones in your relationship, each with:
   - the month and year
   - a short title
   - two or three sentences about it
   - a photo, if you have one
10. How did the proposal happen (or how did the families decide)?
11. Is there an object, place or inside joke that keeps coming up in your story?

## 3. Families

We introduce your families to guests who may not know everyone. For **each side**, please list the people you'd like featured (around 5 to 10 per side):

12. Name, and their relation to the bride or groom (for example "Bride's maternal uncle (Ammavan)").
13. One friendly line about them (for example "Runs a spice shop in Mattancherry; the sadya pickles are his").
14. A photo of each person (optional, but lovely).
15. Anyone who has passed away whom you would like to remember on the website?
16. Your native places and family backgrounds, as you'd like them described (for example "a Kochi family and a Coimbatore family").

## 4. Wedding events

For **each event** (engagement, haldi or mehendi, wedding or muhurtham, reception, and any others):

17. Event name.
18. Date, start time and end time.
19. Venue name and full address.
20. A Google Maps link to the venue.
21. Dress code or colour suggestions.
22. One or two lines about what happens at this event.

For the main ceremony:

23. Exact muhurtham time.
24. What time should guests be seated?

## 5. Ceremonies and traditions

Many guests may be attending a South Indian wedding for the first time. We explain each ritual simply.

25. Which ceremonies will take place? (For example Kanyadanam, Thali tying, Saptapadi, Malai maatral or garland exchange, Kasi Yatra, Sadya.)
26. Any family-specific traditions or customs you'd like explained?
27. Is there anything guests should know or do during a ritual (for example showering akshata, staying seated)?
28. Anything we should avoid mentioning, or describe in a particular way?

## 6. Venue and guest information

29. Parking: where, how many cars, valet or overflow parking?
30. Restrooms and baby-changing facilities.
31. Accessibility: ramps, lifts, wheelchairs, seating for elders.
32. Food timings (welcome drinks, sadya sittings, tea, dinner).
33. Two or three helpline contacts for guests (name and phone number), for example one person from each side.
34. Travel or stay suggestions for outstation guests (nearby hotels, airport or railway station distance).

## 7. RSVP

35. Do you want guests to RSVP on the website? By what date?
36. Meal options to offer (for example vegetarian sadya, non-vegetarian, Jain, kids).
37. Anything else to ask guests (for example accommodation needed, arrival date)?

## 8. Bride vs Groom game

The game gets both families mingling. Guests join Team Bride or Team Groom and earn points on the big screen.

38. **Couple quiz:** 8 to 10 questions about you, each with four options, the correct answer, and a one-line fun explanation. (For example "Where did we first meet?")
39. **Predictions:** 3 to 5 playful "who will…" questions with options (for example "Who will cry first during the thali tying?", "Which sweet disappears first?").
40. **Photo challenges:** any tasks you'd like guests to try (for example "Selfie with someone from the other family").
41. Any family members who are happy to be named in the game (for example as team captains)?

## 9. Photos, films and music

42. A portrait of each of you (good quality, portrait orientation if possible).
43. 5 to 10 favourite photos of you together, for the story and the slideshow.
44. A save-the-date or pre-wedding video, if you have one.
45. A song for the website's background music (YouTube link or audio file). Soft instrumental music works best.
46. Should guest photos appear on the website immediately, or only after a family member approves them?

## 10. After the wedding

47. A thank-you message to guests (we show this after the wedding).
48. Will you have highlight films or a wedding film to add later?
49. Would you like a time capsule, where guests write messages you open on your first anniversary?

## 11. Look and feel

50. Colours you love, or your wedding colour theme (for example ivory and gold kasavu, or blush and rose gold).
51. Words that describe the feel you want: traditional, modern, minimal, festive, elegant, playful?
52. Any websites, invitations or designs you've seen and liked?
53. Which languages should the website offer (English, Malayalam, Tamil, others)?

## 12. Practical details

54. Who from the family will manage the website on the day (approving photos and running the game)? Name and phone number.
55. Should the website be private (shared only through your invitation QR code) or open to anyone with the link?
56. Your preferred web address, if you'd like a custom one (for example karthikanjali.in).
57. Anything you definitely do **not** want on the website?

---

**How to send your answers:** reply in writing or with voice notes, whichever is easier. Send photos as original files (not WhatsApp-compressed) through Google Drive or a similar link.

Thank you! We can't wait to bring your story to life.
